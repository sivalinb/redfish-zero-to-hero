# Independent transfer challenge

After completing Level 10, close the worked examples and produce an evidence-based report for this new challenge. Do not ask the tutor to write the final report for you. You may consult primary documentation and collect new evidence.

A new node advertises only On and GracefulShutdown. A host service is unavailable while the BMC responds. Discover supported resources; distinguish host state, BMC reachability, inventory, and health. Explain why a GracefulRestart copied from another node may fail. Produce a read-only inventory with explicit missing fields and a response plan tied to observed evidence.

## What to submit

State the question and environment, list assumptions, include reproducible commands or calculations, attach actual output, explain the relevant units and relationships, justify your diagnosis or plan, and distinguish observed results from unknowns. Include a verification step and a suitable next experiment.

## Manual review rubric

Award 0, 1, or 2 points for each: terminology in your own words; reproducible evidence; correct reasoning with units and identities; adaptation to changed conditions; honest verification and limits. A mentor should review a score of at least 8/10 with no material unsafe or unsupported claim before independent practice. This manual exercise does not automatically grant a badge or certification.

The automated course badges demonstrate the included objectives. This challenge is an additional transfer check, not evidence that an actual learner has passed it.
